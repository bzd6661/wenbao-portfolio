Project 01

Private Housing

Studio · Netherlands · 90 m² · Under Construction

01 — Parametric process
The roof reads as complex, yet follows simple rules: a chopped cube, duplicated four times and rotated 90/180° — the high points connected into roof lines, kept live in Grasshopper.
02 — Physical model
The final geometry leaves the screen: a laser-cut lattice roof, hand-assembled over the cast interior volumes.

Project 02

Rijksdorp Tourist Center

Visitor Centre · Leiden, NL · 26 m² · Shortlisted, Young Architecture Award

01 — Parametric process
Inspired by Kengo Kuma: stone-like volumes grown through mesh subdivision, meant to sit back in harmony with the dune and forest landscape rather than dominate it.
02 — Physical model
The subdivided meshes are unrolled and folded back into space — paper solids testing the movable volumes against the human figure.
